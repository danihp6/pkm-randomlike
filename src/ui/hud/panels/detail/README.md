# `src/ui/hud/panels/detail/` — the member detail card (`03m`)

The route's member detail window as Preact over the `#hud` layer (`D49`): the shipped
460 × 560 card — the name, level, status and type chips, the STATS / MOVES tabs, the six stats
with their nature tint, the evolution graph, the 2x2 move grid, the learnset search field and
pool, and the rename field — with the board's paint from `03m Panels · pokemon moves`. The scene
module `src/scenes/panels/detail.ts` keeps the shipped layout arithmetic, the panel record and the
run, and feeds this panel's view, so a turn of `?hud=dom` swaps the drawing and not the rules.

## Layout

- `view.ts` — the panel's value model: `detailPanelView` (a pure projection of the member, the learnset pool and the panel's cursor state), `detailPanelBox`, `initialDetailPanelUi`, the block of mirrored Phaser constants and the close control's own view (`close`, `DETAIL_CLOSE_SIZE`, `detailCloseKey`). A move slot carries its own `box`, its ⓘ and the five boxes of the cell: the type badge's and the category glyph's 24 px squares in the left column, and the name, PP and power / accuracy lines beside them. The info band carries both ability texts (`abilityDesc`, `ability2Desc`) and one ⓘ whose `abilityInfo` exists when either of them does.
- `view.test.ts` — the mirrored constants against their literals, both panel boxes, the tabs, the six stats and their value inset, the four move cells with their ⓘ and their five label boxes (the 116 px / 73 px name line, the icon column clear of every text line), the learnset window and its page clamp, the cursor clamps, purity and the empty view, the pair of ability texts with the ⓘ appearing for the second alone and staying away when neither has text, the close control's one box on every tab and viewport and `detailCloseKey`'s truth table, headless.
- `evolution.ts` — the evolution graph as SVG data: one row per family node at the shipped indent and pitch, one elbow path string per branch, and the pool / window the band reads and scrolls its family through.
- `evolution.test.ts` — the row origins, the pool and its window, the one-row scroll with its clamp, the exact `d` strings of the `03m` family and of its Mega / Gmax branches, the Eevee family's ten rows, the card fit and the two constants the scene shares with this module, headless.
- `detailPanel.tsx` — the card itself: the header, the chips, the rename field, the tab strip, the stats, the info band with its ability ⓘ (whose accessible name carries both descriptions and whose pinned state adds one out-of-flow line per ability that has text), the SVG evolution graph, the move grid with its two atlas squares per cell and its ⓘ buttons, the search field with its page arrows and the pool, whose rows carry the type badge instead of the type word.
- `detail.css` — the board's card and cell paint — radius 11, the 1 px highlight, the 26 px corner badge, the accent current/ready tones — over the `styles/root.css` tokens, plus the `#4ad15c` positive the token sheet does not carry and the absolutely positioned ability-description block the pinned ⓘ adds.
- `mount.tsx` — `mountDetailPanel`: renders the card into a host element against the scene's view signal, inside the panel frame that carries the saved offset (the board draws no chip for this window), and returns its disposer. It also owns the card's **way back**: one `keydown` in the document's capture phase calls `detailCloseKey` and sends `{ type: 'close' }`, so an `Escape` closes the card before the route's keybinds can open the pause menu — and while a text field holds the focus, `Escape` stays that field's own cancel.
- `fixture.test.ts` — the `design/fixtures/03m-panels-pokemon-moves.json` frame rebuilt headlessly: its `HudState` validates, the header and the four move cards, the learnset band, the pasted family graph and the two-ability pin (one line per ability, both texts on the ⓘ's label).
- `specGap.test.ts` — the card's moves-tab copy measured against the imported `03m` screen spec through `specGap.views/detail.ts`: the empty box and colour claims, the eighteen strings it prints and the board carries, the level line the card types `Lv36` where the board draws `LV 36`, and the 460 × 560 card's geometry against the board's 360 × 508.
- `index.ts` — the barrel the scene module imports.
- `README.md` — this map.

## Rules

1. The view is data, never the run: no scene, no engine, no save, no RNG, no clock. The fixture's `state` is the empty `HudState` because this panel reads a `MemberDetail`, not a `HudState` — exactly as `02-options` carries its own `panel` block.
2. The panel answers in `DetailIntent`s, its own command vocabulary, which does **not** extend `HudCommand`: the detail window's interaction set (a move slot's grab and drop, a learnset equip, a rename) has no equivalent in that union. `src/scenes/panels/detail.ts` is the only receiver and the only writer of the run; every write goes through the engine functions that module already imports and lands through `commitRun`.
3. The card is `position: fixed` at `detailPanelBox(viewport)` and every inner rect is panel-relative, so the shipped dock, its clamp on a phone and the 1 px gate all come from one arithmetic.
4. `open` and the search query are the scene's to stamp after the pure projection (the frame machinery owns the collapse, the scene owns the filter), so the model never carries a second copy of either.
5. The evolution graph is SVG: one `<path>` per edge, one `<text>` per known level and the names as HTML over it, so a press anywhere on a ready row lands on its `<g role="button">`. The portrait cell is a placeholder rect — the board's sprite is the canvas's.
6. Every control is a real button or input and carries the kit's 44 × 44 hit overlay; a press is a click. A move cell is `cursor` on one press and `grab` (or `drop` on an empty slot) on a double press.
7. A paused route (a battle over it, the Settings screen) hides the card: `src/scenes/panels/detail.ts` follows the scene's own `PAUSE`/`RESUME` beats and stamps `open: false`, so the card never floats over the battle, and the resume gives back the same rect and the same tab.
8. The card is the one route panel with **no strip badge**, so it carries its own way back: the 26 px chip at its top-left corner is a real `button` (`data-hud-detail-close`, glyph `×`, accessible name `t('keybind.cancel')`) wired to `{ type: 'close' }`, and `Escape` does the same through the mount's capture-phase listener. The frame still draws no chip for it (`chipLetter('detail') === ''`), and no second control is added.
9. The ⓘ's pinned block is one line per ability that has text, absolutely positioned under the info row (`data-hud-detail="ability-desc"`), so the frame `03m` — which carries no second-ability box — gains a row by CSS rather than by a new rect: every measured box of the card stays where `specGap.test.ts` records it, and the block does not exist at all while the ⓘ is unpinned.
